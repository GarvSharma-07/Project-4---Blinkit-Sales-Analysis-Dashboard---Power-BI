# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project designed to analyze sales performance, outlet characteristics, product categories, customer ratings, and outlet location types.

The dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful visual insights, helping stakeholders understand business performance, identify high-performing product categories, and compare outlet-level metrics for better decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance and average sales.
- Evaluate outlet performance based on establishment year and outlet size.
- Understand product demand across different item categories.
- Compare sales of Low Fat and Regular fat-content products.
- Analyze customer ratings and item visibility.
- Compare sales performance across different outlet location tiers.
- Build an interactive dashboard for data-driven decision-making.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis
- Displays total sales and average sales.
- Tracks outlet establishment year against total sales.
- Highlights changes in sales performance over time.

### 2. Fat Content Analysis
- Compares sales of Low Fat and Regular products.
- Uses a doughnut chart to visualize their contribution to total sales.
- Includes outlet-level comparisons by fat content.

### 3. Item Type Analysis
- Compares sales across product categories.
- Identifies leading categories such as Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods.
- Helps highlight categories contributing significantly to revenue.

### 4. Outlet Size Analysis
- Compares sales across High, Medium, and Small outlet sizes.
- Uses a doughnut chart to show the sales contribution of each outlet size.

### 5. Outlet Location Analysis
- Compares performance across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 locations.
- Helps identify location tiers contributing the most to total sales.

### 6. Outlet Performance Table
Provides a detailed comparison of:
- Outlet Type
- Total Sales
- Average Sales
- Average Rating
- Number of Items
- Item Visibility

### 7. Interactive Filters
The dashboard includes filters for:
- **Outlet Type**
- **Outlet Size**
- **Item Type**

These filters allow users to explore different segments of the business.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

Based on the displayed dashboard:

- **Tier 3 outlets** contribute the highest sales among the three location tiers, at approximately 472.1K.
- **Medium-sized outlets** show the highest sales contribution among the displayed outlet-size segments, at approximately 507.9K.
- **Fruits & Vegetables** is the leading item category, generating approximately 178K in sales.
- **Snack Foods** ranks among the top-performing categories, with approximately 175K in sales.
- The dashboard reports an **average customer rating of 3.92**, providing a useful indicator of customer experience.
- The outlet establishment year chart shows a sales peak of approximately 205K around 2018, followed by lower sales in subsequent years.

*Note: Figures are approximate and based on the dashboard visual. Findings may vary when filters are applied.*
